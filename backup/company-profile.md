# Borgo in Fiore — Company Profile

## What this company is

Borgo in Fiore is a Mediterranean dining and celebrations business in Terlizzi, serving guests and hosts across Puglia. It brings people together through thoughtful food, welcoming spaces, and warm, memorable hospitality for shared meals and meaningful gatherings.

## Current objective

Build the public Borgo in Fiore presence with the Terlizzi/Puglia location, clear Mediterranean dining and celebrations positioning, confirmed contact details, menu and event inquiry calls to action, owner-supplied dining and celebration information, and an event inquiry flow.

## Who it serves

Guests and event hosts in Terlizzi and across Puglia, including people planning weddings, receptions, corporate gatherings, birthdays, pizza-focused celebrations, or everyday Mediterranean meals.

## Offerings

- Mediterranean dining
- Celebrations and private events

## Voice

Warm, welcoming, personal, beautiful, and rooted in Mediterranean hospitality and the spirit of Puglia.

## Quick brand summary

- Business type: Mediterranean dining and celebrations
- Location: Terlizzi, Puglia
- Core promise: thoughtful food, warm hospitality, memorable shared experiences
- Main audiences: everyday diners and event hosts
- Brand tone: warm, personal, elegant, and rooted in local Mediterranean culture
